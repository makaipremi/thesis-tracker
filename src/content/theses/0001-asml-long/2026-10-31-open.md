My view on ASML after its Q3 results.

**Demand is running ahead of supply, and management says so.** Q3 revenue was €11.7bn, up about 55% year on year, and Q4 guidance of €14.2–15.2bn implies roughly another 50%. That puts 2026 at around €44–45bn (+36%). On the results video the CEO said that adding 30% capacity in 2027 and again in 2028 "will not allow us to meet our customer demand", and some customers are already asking to place orders for 2029 and even 2030.

**The read-across from its customers confirms it.** TSMC, NVIDIA and Micron all describe demand ahead of supply with no date for balance. Memory is the tightest spot: ASML expects its sales to memory chipmakers to rise about 75% this year.

**The balance sheet de-risks the story.** ASML is net cash (~€10bn), spends only about 5% of sales on capex, and its customers are chipmakers rather than AI labs. Customer down payments exceed its receivables, so it isn't financing the AI build-out the way some peers are.

**And it's cheaper than a year ago.** The shares are up ~80% in twelve months, but earnings expectations rose faster, so it trades below its own five-year average forward multiple.

**What would change my mind:** the main risk is execution on customers' side — management itself flags that 2027–28 fab construction must be managed "very diligently". A sector-wide AI sell-off would hit ASML too in the short term, but I expect quality names like this to recover first.
